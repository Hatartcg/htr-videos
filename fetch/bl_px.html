Description

Gear Up for Mega-Evolutions and Stroll Through the Streets of Lumiose City!

The Mega-Evolution: Perfect Order Three-Blister Pack (ME03) is back! Highly favored by both Trainers and collectors, this classic format offers the ideal balance between a thrilling multi-pack opening and an exclusive collector’s item to display.

The Perfect Order expansion is the absolute sensation of Spring 2026. Echoing the highly anticipated release of the Pokémon Legends: Z-A video game, this series brings the Kalos region back into the spotlight by reintroducing a fan-favorite, explosive gameplay mechanic: Mega-Evolutions. Whether you are looking to strengthen your deck or hunt down the finest rarities, this 3-booster blister is a must-have.

Why Choose the 3-Pack Blister Format?

  • For Pack Openers: With 3 booster packs, you significantly increase your chances of pulling the expansion’s top chase cards, such as the breathtaking Special Illustration Rares (SAR).

  • For Sealed Collectors: Protected by its sturdy blister shell, this product beautifully showcases its exclusive promo card, making it a premier piece for any display shelf or showcase.

Hunting for the Treasures of Perfect Order

Inside your packs, you might just cross paths with some of the most sought-after cards on the market:

  • Mega-Zygarde-ex: The legendary titan in its most formidable state.

  • Mega-Starmie-ex & Mega-Clefable-ex: Iconic first-generation Pokémon returning to grace with breathtaking artwork.

  • Meowth: The unofficial mascot of the set, featured in highly coveted alternate art prints.

The Perfect Order Three-Pack Blister includes:

  • 3 Pokémon TCG booster packs from the Perfect Order (ME03) expansion

  • 1 exclusive holographic promo card featuring Chikorita

  • Language: French 🇫🇷 (Note: Kept as French since the text specifies the box itself is the French version!
