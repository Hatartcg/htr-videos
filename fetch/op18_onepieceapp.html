What the numbers say about OP18

OP18 sells for about $218 sealed, 82% above the $120 MSRP. Supply has tightened without going scarce, which usually means print is finished and stock is still working through the channel.

The expected value of opening one is $859 against a $218 sealed price, close to break-even by our numbers. Read that as a reason to check the working rather than a green light: EV is an average over many boxes and says nothing about the one in front of you.

Franky (Alternate Art) (OP18-021) is the most valuable card in OP18 at $200, on its own 38% of the set's entire card value. The top fifteen cards carry 72% of the set's total value: a handful of chases, and a long tail that funds very little.

Broken down by what you actually pull, alternate-art cards account for 47% of a box's expected return, at about 2 per box. That is the line the set's economics rest on, and it is why box results feel streaky: miss it and the rest of the box does not come close to covering the cost.
