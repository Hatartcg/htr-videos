Riot Games Reveals Riftbound: Radiance During Vancouver Regional Qualifier

By Alex Swift Posted: June 1, 2026 1:48 PM EDT

Riot Games has officially unveiled Riftbound: Radiance, the fifth set for the Riftbound Trading Card Game, during the Vancouver Regional Qualifier broadcast.

Radiance focuses on champions who solve problems through ingenuity, creativity, and spectacular plays across Runeterra. Riot also announced that the set will be the first Riftbound release to launch simultaneously in English, Simplified Chinese, and French. So no more sneaking AI translations of Chinese cards before the North American release.

Several cards were showcased during the reveal, including:

  • Evelynn, In Control

  • Ekko, Ingenious

Advertisement
  • Seraphine, Not Alone (full card reveal)

Riot also confirmed several upcoming products for the set:

Booster Display
Featuring Seraphine, Ekko, Ziggs, and Jarvan.

Showdown Deck
A ready-to-play Evelynn vs. Seraphine deck.

Advertisement

Radiance Vault
Includes six Radiance booster packs, a rune pack, and three double-sided full-art tokens.

Pre-Rift Event Kits
Featuring nine different champion preconstructed decks.

Players can expect more reveals during Preview Season, which runs from September 28 through October 9, 2026. Pre-Rift events will follow from October 16 through October 22, with the global release of Radiance scheduled for October 23, 2026.

With new champions, new products, and the game’s first multi-language release parity, Radiance is shaping up to be another major step forward for Riftbound.
