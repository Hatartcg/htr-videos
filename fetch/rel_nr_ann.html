Announcement: NARUTO Mythos TCG – Set 2 is Coming!

Update – Important changes to the Set 2 release plan

The pre-release weekend scheduled for August 28, 29 and 30 will not take place. We're sorry for this change: we know many players and stores were already organising around those dates.

Set 2 (1st Edition) launches worldwide on September 4 as planned, with the Booster Display. The Special Pack Design A has moved to the second wave in October (exact date still to be confirmed). See the updated timeline below.

We’re excited to officially reveal that Set 2: Shinobi Shiren will be arriving this September! This new set brings a lot of fresh ideas, improvements, and collectible highlights that we can’t wait to share with you.

Numbered Cards

We received amazing feedback on the numbered cards from Set 1, where we introduced 4 alternate secret rare cards limited to 75 copies each.

With Set 2, we’re taking this concept even further - introducing a more refined and structured numbering system. This will not only enhance long-term collectible value but also give collectors even more high-value cards to chase.

Gold Cards

Our most premium cards are making a return. Featuring real 22k gold, fine micro-engraving, limited production, and unique numbering, Gold Cards will once again be part of Set 2. These are true centerpiece items for collectors looking to expand their premium collection.

Original Designs (Big News!)

This is one of the biggest updates in Set 2: we’re introducing original card designs into the collection.

These designs will appear across different rarities, meaning:

  • Some will be more accessible, so more fans can enjoy them
  • Others will be highly limited, numbered cards with significant collector value

We know that extremely limited cards (like runs of 50, 250, or 500) can be tough to obtain, so we wanted to strike a better balance. That’s why we’re also introducing a new rarity tier for special designs, with a defined pull rate. This keeps cards exciting to chase while still giving more players a fair shot at owning something unique.

New Rarities

﻿Set 2 will introduce several new rarity levels, not just for original designs, but across the entire set. This adds more depth to collecting and keeps the experience fresh and engaging for both players and collectors.

NARUTO Mythos TCG – Set 2 (1st Edition) Release Timeline

September 4 – Official Global Release

Set 2 (1st Edition) becomes widely available in stores:

  • NARUTO Mythos TCG Set 2, 1st edition Booster display

October – Second Wave Release (dates still to be confirmed)

  • NARUTO Mythos TCG Set 2, 1st edition Special pack design A
  • NARUTO Mythos TCG Set 2, 1st edition Special pack design B
  • NARUTO Mythos TCG Set 2, 1st edition Team Set

Please note: a number of Set 2 cards become playable only with the release of the Special Pack Design A. As this product is part of the second wave, those cards will not be available on September 4.

For Our OP Community:

The pre-release weekend planned for August 28, 29 and 30 will not take place, and pre-release tournaments will not be held. We sincerely apologise to the stores and players who were preparing for those days.

Set 2 (1st Edition) will be available from September 4, the official global release date.

Thank you for your patience and support — we'll share any further updates on the second wave as soon as they are confirmed.
