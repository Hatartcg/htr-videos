New Naruto Mythos TCG on the way

An international project to bring the world of Naruto to the table

Written by Ross Locksley on 03 Feb 2026


The Naruto Mythos Trading Card Game has been given a release date of March 13th, 2026 for Europe, the UK and Turkey.  The game made its debut at Spiel Essen 2025 and has since been tested at events such as Milan Games Week & Cartoomics 2025 and the London Toy Fair. 

Given the success of the Gundam TCG, it's good to see another anime property making a bold stride into the card gaming space. The cards are looking good, large faces and some nice graphical details such as the scrolls. As for how it plays... we'll find out in March. Retailers are being invited to take part in organised tournaments so keep an eye out, or let your local high street games store know you'd like to play.

From the Press Release:

The TCG will debut with its first official expansion, Konoha Shidō 木ノ葉始動, a set that immediately takes players into the heart of the Hidden Leaf Village. The core collection features cards divided into Commons, Uncommons, Rares, Secrets and a Legendary card with 22-karat gold foil, conceived as a true collector’s piece. On top of that, players will find: 

  • ART Rares with micro-engraved holographic finishes
  • Secret Variants with extremely limited print runs
  • Mythos special cards, distributed exclusively through events, tournaments and promotional products

Each card is designed as a fragment of story: not only a gameplay element, but a tangible piece of Naruto’s narrative universe, meant to be collected, played and experienced.
