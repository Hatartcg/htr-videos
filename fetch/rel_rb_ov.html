New Set, New Mechanics

Deploy

Play this Gear only to a Battlefield and it will grant benefits while there. When an opponent holds that Battlefield, kill this Gear.

Show Off

Reveal, or “Show Off,” a qualifying card from your hand or on your board to unlock a new effect of the played card.

Disarm

Disarm reduces the Might of an enemy Unit by as you attack!

Disarm is similar to Assault. Each instance of Disarm will make its Might reduction stronger.

New Types of Cards

New Unit Type: Equipment Units

Gear Units were introduced in Vendetta with the Patched Porobot. With Orianna’s Legend effect, equipment can now become Units as well.

Before you get too excited about equipping Units onto one another— when an Equipment becomes a Unit, it’s automatically unequipped.

New Tokens

Bomb

Bombs are small gifts to leave your opponent! The more bombs you play, the bigger the boom. 

Collectibility

Ultimate Rarity:

Radiance brings Riftbound’s second Ultimate Rare.

We learned a lot with the Ultimate Rare introduced in Unleashed. While finding one was very exciting, it wasn’t quite as rare as we wanted it to be. We're trying something new with this card: it's our rarest Ultimate Rare yet, significantly harder to find than the Baron.

Booster Pack Rarity Distribution

Each Riftbound: Radiance booster pack contains:

  • 7 Commons
  • 3 Uncommons
  • 1 Foil slot that can be a Common, Uncommon, Rare or Epic
  • 1 Rare or Epic
  • 1 slot that can be a Rare, Epic, Alt Art, Special Alt, Overnumber, or Ultimate Rare
  • 1 Token, Rune, or Alt Art Rune
  • 1 informational insert

Each pack can contain at most one Ultimate Rare card. There is no guaranteed Ultimate Rare in any pack, box, or case. On average across the entire Radiance print run, an Ultimate Rare appears in approximately 0.025% of booster packs. Pack contents are randomized during manufacturing. Any stated appearance rates reflect averages across the print run and are not guarantees of appearance in booster packs or booster displays.
