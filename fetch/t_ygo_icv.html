NEW 'YU-GI-OH! TCG' BOOSTER SET REVEALED
'Glorious Victors'
Posted by Jeffrey Dohm-Sanchez on August 18, 2026 @ 11:39 am CT

Konami Digital Entertainment revealed Glorious Victors, a new booster set for Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG, which will hit retail on December 4, 2026.

The new 60-card booster set introduces the Zenet strategy for decks where Duelists' favorite Normal Monsters fight alongside would-be Egyptian kings. This strategy allows players' Normal Monsters to support their Ritual Monsters in the afterlife (from the Graveyard). The set also contains monsters from the Trirealm Rift, which are creatures that defy the laws of physics.

The new set has 3 Secret Rares, 10 Ultra Rares, 10 Super Rares, and 37 Rares. Additionally, 15 cards are also available as Collector’s Rares and 10 cards are available as Starlight Rares. Packs come with 7 cards and retail for $4.49.
