MEDIA ALERT: Mega Greninja Faces Off with Mega Floette in the Pokémon Trading Card Game: Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising Expansion, Available Now

May 22, 2026 — The Pokémon Company International released the latest expansion for the best-selling Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG), Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising, available today at participating retailers around the world.
 
In Pokémon TCG: Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising, Mega Floette ex wreaks havoc as night falls in Lumiose City. Players can leverage Mega Greninja ex and other powerful Pokémon, including Mega Pyroar ex and Mega Dragalge ex, to ward off this dangerous threat.
 
Furthermore, Trainers will encounter more Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, appearing as ultra rare cards that feature silhouettes of their appearances before Mega Evolution in the artwork. 
 
Trainers can discover and collect cards from the Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising expansion through booster packs, Elite Trainer Boxes and various collections at participating retailers worldwide beginning today.
 
Additionally, Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising is now available digitally via the Pokémon TCG Live game on iOS, Android, macOS and Windows. Players can collect and battle with new Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, and upon login, the Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising Battle Pass will reward players with a new deck featuring Mega Greninja ex. An additional deck featuring Mega Floette ex can be unlocked with further play.[1] Trainers can also sharpen their skills with the newly launched Build & Battle mode in Pokémon TCG Live, which allows players to battle against one another with a deck built from a randomly selected 40-card deck and four booster packs.

For more information about the Pokémon TCG, please visit Pokemon.com/TCG.
 

 
[1] Players can unlock the Mega Evolution—Chaos Rising Battle Pass through 6 p.m. BST on July 16, 2026.
